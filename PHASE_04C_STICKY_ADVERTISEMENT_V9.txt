Dalil Al Hami - Phase 04C sticky advertisement enhancement v9
================================================================

- The advertisement starts as the existing full-size panel.
- It collapses into a compact pinned banner while the user scrolls.
- It expands again when the user returns to the top.
- The pinned banner remains part of the scroll layout and does not cover cards.
- The layout adapts to narrow screens and enlarged system text.
- Pull-to-refresh and PageStorage scroll preservation remain enabled.
- Supabase, authentication, business cards, and navigation are unchanged.
