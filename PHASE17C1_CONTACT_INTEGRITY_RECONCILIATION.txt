The two known legacy phone mismatches are old imported businesses whose
businesses.phone still contains concatenated historic phone strings while the
new business_contact_numbers rows already contain the correct primary number.

Scope
=====
1. Preserve business_contact_numbers as the authoritative source.
2. Add an explicit internal projection-write guard so updating
   businesses.phone / businesses.whatsapp from contact rows can never trigger
   destructive legacy re-parsing.
3. Reconcile legacy phone/WhatsApp columns from active contact rows.
4. Align client/server validation to 5..20 numeric digits.
5. Add hard migration postconditions for:
   - 1..5 active contacts
   - exactly one primary
   - at most one WhatsApp
   - 5..20 numeric digits
   - no normalized duplicates
   - zero legacy projection mismatches
